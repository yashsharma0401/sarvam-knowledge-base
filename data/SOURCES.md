# Data attribution and pinning

The bundled archive contains a deterministic subset of HotpotQA, including its Wikipedia excerpts and original labels. Credit to Zhilin Yang, Peng Qi, Saizheng Zhang, Yoshua Bengio, William W. Cohen, Ruslan Salakhutdinov, and Christopher D. Manning, and to the Wikipedia contributors of the source articles. This derivative selects and compresses records and retains their titles, text, labels, and sentence coordinates. HotpotQA and this derivative data are distributed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). The implementation is separate from the dataset. [HotpotQA paper](https://aclanthology.org/D18-1259/) · [Project and data license](https://github.com/hotpotqa/hotpot)
